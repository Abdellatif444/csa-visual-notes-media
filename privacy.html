Privacy Policy — Visual Notes Publisher

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Visual Notes Publisher is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Abdellatif Gourri. It is used only to publish the operator's own study notes (images and videos) to the operator's own Pinterest and Instagram accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • Only the operator's own Pinterest and Instagram business accounts, after the operator explicitly authorizes the app.
  • On Pinterest: the operator's boards (to choose where to publish) and the Pins the app creates. Basic analytics of those Pins may be read to track the operator's own content performance.
  • The app does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest or Instagram user.

How data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets of a private GitHub repository and are never published.
  • The app keeps a private log of the posts it published (title, date, link). No personal data of other users is kept.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Before publishing, the text and image of each post (the operator's own content) may be sent to an AI proofreading service (Google Gemini API) to detect mistakes. No user data is sent.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings (Security → Apps) or Instagram settings (Apps and websites). On revocation, the stored tokens are deleted.
