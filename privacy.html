Privacy Policy — Visual Notes Publisher

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Visual Notes Publisher is a personal tool operated by Abdellatif Gourri. It publishes only the operator's own content (IT study notes, and the printable coloring pages of the operator's Etsy shop Pagewhim) to the operator's own accounts: Pinterest, Instagram and the Facebook Page "Pagewhim". It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Only the operator's own Pinterest accounts, Instagram professional accounts and Facebook Page, after the operator explicitly authorizes the app.
  • Publishing: the boards, Pins, posts and stories the app creates on those accounts.
  • Analytics: basic statistics of the operator's own accounts and posts (views, saves, clicks, follower count).
  • Comments: the text of comments that people leave under the operator's own posts, and the name shown with them, are read once a day so the operator can answer quickly. The app never replies, likes, sends messages or contacts anyone; the operator answers by hand.
  • The app does not read followers' profiles, private messages, or any data outside the operator's own posts.

How data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets of a private GitHub repository and are never published.
  • The app keeps a private log of the posts it published (title, date, link) and a private summary of new comments (category, short summary, suggested answer). Comment summaries are used only by the operator to reply.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Google Gemini API is used to proofread each post before publication (the operator's own text and image) and to sort new comments (question, request, problem, compliment, spam). No other data is sent.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings (Security → Apps), Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or Facebook settings (Business integrations). On revocation, the stored tokens are deleted.

Anyone who commented on one of the operator's posts can ask for the summary of their comment to be deleted by opening an issue at github.com/Abdellatif444 or by sending a message to the Facebook Page "Pagewhim". The summary is deleted within 7 days and the deletion is confirmed.
